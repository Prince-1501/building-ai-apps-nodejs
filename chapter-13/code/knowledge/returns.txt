Return Policy

We offer a 30-day return policy for all products purchased from our store. Items must be in their original condition with tags attached and original packaging. To start a return, visit your account page and select the order you want to return, then click "Request Return" and follow the instructions.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. The refund will be issued to the original payment method. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item was defective or we made an error.

For defective items, we offer a full refund including shipping costs, or a free replacement. Please contact support within 48 hours of receiving a defective item and include photos of the defect.

Digital products and gift cards cannot be returned or refunded. Sale items marked as "Final Sale" are also not eligible for returns.

If your return is outside the 30-day window, we may offer store credit on a case-by-case basis. Please contact our support team to discuss your situation.
