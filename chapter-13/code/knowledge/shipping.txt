Shipping Information

We offer three shipping options for domestic orders:

Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days, free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99. Economy Shipping: 7-10 business days, $2.99 for all orders. Express Shipping: 1-2 business days, $12.99 for all orders.

International shipping is available to over 50 countries. International orders typically take 10-21 business days depending on the destination. International shipping rates start at $14.99 and vary by weight and destination. Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer.

All orders include a tracking number sent via email once the order ships. You can track your order on our website by entering the tracking number on the Order Tracking page. Orders placed before 2 PM EST on business days are shipped the same day.

If your order has not arrived within the expected delivery window, please check the tracking information first. If the tracking shows the package as delivered but you have not received it, contact us within 7 days and we will investigate.

We currently do not offer same-day delivery. For urgent orders, Express Shipping with 1-2 business day delivery is the fastest option available.
